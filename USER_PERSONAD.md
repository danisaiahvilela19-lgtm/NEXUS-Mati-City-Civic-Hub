# NEXUS: Mati City Civic Hub
## User Personas

*SDG Alignment:* SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities

These are preliminary fictional personas created to guide the design of NEXUS. They should be validated and updated through user interviews.

## 1. Concerned Resident

*Name:* Lawrence Jason Legaspi  
*Age:* 22  
*Occupation:* College Student  
*Role:* Primary User

### About
A resident of Mati City who wants a convenient way to report community issues and monitor the progress of submitted reports.

### Goals
- Report community problems easily.
- Track the status of submitted reports.
- Receive updates about reported concerns.

### Frustrations
- Not knowing where to report community issues.
- Difficulty following up on complaints.
- Uncertainty about whether a report has been received.

### Needs
- Simple community issue reporting.
- A report status tracking feature.
- Clear and accessible updates.

### How NEXUS Helps
NEXUS aims to provide a simple way to report community issues and check their progress.

## 2. Environmental Volunteer

*Name:* Jestoni B. Mabandos  
*Age:* 23  
*Occupation:* Community Volunteer  
*Role:* Secondary User

### About
A community member interested in environmental protection and activities that help maintain a clean and safe community.

### Goals
- Discover cleanup drives and environmental programs.
- Find information about upcoming activities.
- Participate in community initiatives.

### Frustrations
- Missing announcements about activities.
- Difficulty finding event details.

### Needs
- An environmental initiatives page.
- Activity schedules and participation information.

### How NEXUS Helps
NEXUS aims to make environmental initiatives and community activities easier to discover.

## 3. LGU Response Officer

*Name:* Anna Marie C. Columna  
*Age:* 49  
*Occupation:* Local Government Staff Member  
*Role:* Stakeholder Persona

### About
A potential staff user responsible for reviewing community concerns and providing updates on their progress.

### Goals
- Review submitted community reports.
- Update report statuses.
- Communicate progress to residents.

### Frustrations
- Incomplete report details.
- Difficulty organizing incoming reports.
- Repeated requests for status updates.

### Needs
- An authorized report management interface.
- Tools for updating report statuses.
- Organized report information.

### How NEXUS Helps
NEXUS could provide an authorized interface for managing reports and sharing updates, if included in the project scope.

## 4. Research and Validation

These personas are based on the survey conducted with potential users, prioritizing Mati City residents. The feedback gathered was used to identify their needs, goals, and challenges and to refine the personas to better reflect the community's actual experiences.
