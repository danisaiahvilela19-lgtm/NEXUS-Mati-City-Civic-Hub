# NEXUS: Mati City Civic Hub

*SDG Alignment:* SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities

## Project Overview

NEXUS is a proposed civic engagement mobile application designed for Mati City, Davao Oriental. It aims to provide residents with a convenient way to report community issues, monitor the status of their reports, and discover environmental initiatives and community activities.

The project promotes transparency, community participation, and accessible communication between residents and authorized local government personnel.

## Objectives

- Make reporting community issues simple and accessible.
- Allow residents to track the status of submitted reports.
- Provide information about environmental programs and community activities.
- Encourage civic participation in building a cleaner and safer community.

## Target Users

- Residents of Mati City
- Community members and environmental volunteers
- Authorized local government personnel

## Core Features

- *Report an Issue* — Submit concerns such as garbage problems, damaged roads, and broken streetlights.
- *Track My Reports* — View report status and available updates.
- *Environmental Initiatives* — Discover cleanup drives, environmental programs, and community activities.
