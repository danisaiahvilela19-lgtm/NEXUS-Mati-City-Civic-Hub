# AI Disclosure

## Use of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools were used as supporting tools during the development of the NEXUS – Mati City Civic Hub project.

AI assistance was utilized for:
- Brainstorming and improving project ideas.
- Assisting with UI/UX design concepts and layout suggestions.
- Providing guidance on React Native and Expo development.
- Troubleshooting programming errors and improving code readability.
- Assisting in preparing project documentation and explanations.

## Human Contribution and Responsibility

The project developers remained responsible for the planning, design decisions, implementation, testing, and evaluation of the NEXUS application.

All AI-assisted suggestions and outputs were reviewed, modified when necessary, and tested before being incorporated into the project.

## Transparency Statement

AI tools served only as supplementary resources. The developers take responsibility for the final project, its functionality, and its submitted content.
