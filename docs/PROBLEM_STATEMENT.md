# NEXUS: Mati City Civic Hub

*Project Title:* NEXUS: Mati City Civic Hub  
*SDG Alignment:* SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities  
*Project Stage:* Week 1 — Problem Framing

## 1. Problem Background

Residents need accessible ways to communicate community concerns, follow the progress of reported issues, and discover environmental initiatives. NEXUS proposes a centralized mobile application to support civic participation in Mati City, Davao Oriental.

The extent of these challenges in Mati City still needs to be validated through user interviews and research.

## 2. Problem Statement

How might we help residents of Mati City report community issues conveniently, monitor updates from responsible local government offices, and discover environmental initiatives through an accessible mobile application?

## 3. General Objective

To design a civic engagement mobile application that promotes transparent communication, supports community issue reporting, and encourages environmental participation in Mati City.

## 4. Specific Objectives

1. Design a simple process for reporting community issues, aiming for three taps where practical.
2. Provide a feature for residents to view the status and progress of their submitted reports.
3. Share information about environmental initiatives and community activities.
4. Encourage transparency, accountability, and citizen participation in building a cleaner and safer community.

## 5. Target Users

- Residents of Mati City who want to report community concerns.
- Community members and environmental volunteers interested in local initiatives.
- Authorized local government personnel who may review reports and provide status updates.

## 6. Proposed Core Features

### Quick Community Issue Reporting

Allow residents to submit an issue with a category, relevant details, and supporting information.

### Report Status Tracking

Allow residents to check the progress of their submitted reports and view available updates.

### Environmental Initiatives

Provide information about environmental programs, cleanup drives, and other community activities.

## 7. Expected Impact

NEXUS aims to make civic engagement more accessible, improve visibility into report progress, and encourage residents to participate in community and environmental initiatives.

The application cannot guarantee faster government action by itself. Timely status updates depend on participating and authorized personnel.

## 8. Validation Plan

Interview three to five potential users, prioritizing Mati City residents. Ask about their current reporting methods, difficulties following up on concerns, and ways they find information about environmental activities.

Use the findings to revise the problem statement, user personas, and proposed features.
