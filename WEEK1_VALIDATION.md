# NEXUS: Mati City Civic Hub
## Week 1 — Research and Validation

*SDG Alignment:* SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities

### 1. Research Purpose

The research was conducted to understand community concerns, how residents report local issues, how they follow up on complaints, and how they discover environmental activities in Mati City.

### 2. Research Method

A survey was conducted to gather feedback from potential users. The responses were reviewed to identify user needs, challenges, and expectations related to civic engagement.

### 3. Key Findings

The survey responses should be summarized here based on the actual answers collected.

- *Community issue reporting:* Summarize what respondents said about reporting local problems.
- *Report tracking:* Summarize respondents' experiences and expectations regarding updates on reported issues.
- *Environmental initiatives:* Summarize how respondents learn about community activities and environmental programs.
- *Suggested improvements:* Record recurring suggestions or concerns from respondents.

### 4. Impact on Project Design

The research findings will guide the design and prioritization of NEXUS features, including community issue reporting, report status tracking, and environmental initiatives.

### 5. Conclusion

The survey provides initial user feedback to help align NEXUS with the needs of its intended users. The project will use the actual responses to refine its user personas, user flow, and low-fidelity wireframes.
